About

Monkadelic is the creative practice of Daniel Rezinovsky — an Argentinian-Brazilian artist, electronic musician, and consciousness researcher working at the intersection of art, technology, and inner experience.

His work spans generative and algorithmic art, abstract painting, immersive VR environments, audio-reactive visuals, and electronic music production. Central to the practice is a sustained inquiry into the nature of consciousness — informed by over twelve years of clinical psychology, contemplative traditions, and psychedelic integration work.

Current projects include Theosis Project (electronic music), Ontik and Encontro (immersive VR experiences), and Entrementes — a community and workshop platform for neurodivergent creatives, artists, and therapists exploring new modes of expression and learning.

He works with TouchDesigner, p5.js, GLSL shaders, Eurorack synthesis, AI-driven image and video generation, and traditional painting. Based between Brazil and the world.
