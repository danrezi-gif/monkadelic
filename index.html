A little
about this

The person behind the projects.

I'm Daniel Rezinovsky. I make art, music, software, and trouble for the boundaries between them.

PhD candidate, Religious Studies — Rice University
Author, Encontro com o Absoluto
Clinical psychologist — 12+ years
Based between Brazil and the world

I keep moving between generative art, VR, painting, electronic music, psychology, religious study, and AI because I haven't found a reason to separate them. They give me different ways to look at the same questions: what is a mind, and what happens when two minds meet?
